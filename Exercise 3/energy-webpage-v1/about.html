About Us

Helping Australians understand appliance energy use.

PowerWise is a student website created to demonstrate responsive web design, consistent styling and basic JavaScript interactivity.

Our Purpose

Simple energy information

This website presents information about appliance energy consumption in Australia. It is designed to make basic energy concepts easier to understand through clear content and interactive tools.

The site can later be expanded with real appliance datasets, visualisations and more detailed comparisons.

Website features

  • Three connected HTML pages
  • Consistent external CSS styling
  • JavaScript FAQ accordion
  • Interactive energy calculator
  • Responsive navigation

Design Goals

Clear, useful and consistent

Clarity

Information is organised into readable sections with clear headings and simple explanations.

Consistency

The same colours, spacing, navigation and footer design are used across all pages.

Interactivity

JavaScript is used for the FAQ accordion, mobile navigation and appliance energy calculator.
