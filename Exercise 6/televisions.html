Overview

What affects a television's energy use?

Screen Size

Larger screens may require more energy than smaller screens, although actual consumption depends on the model and display technology.

Brightness

Higher brightness settings can increase energy use. Using automatic brightness or energy-saving modes may reduce consumption.

Daily Viewing Time

A television used for many hours each day will generally consume more electricity over a year than one used occasionally.
